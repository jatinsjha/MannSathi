Using your workspace

What can I do as a guest?

Explore the workspace and try the breathing exercise. Sign in to save tasks, calendar events and focus sessions. AI chat is available to approved adult pilot members.

How do I plan my studies?

Open Tasks to add work and due dates, Calendar to schedule events, and Focus Room to start a timed study session. The Dashboard shows the progress saved to your account.

How do classes and teacher accounts work?

Students can request class enrollment under Settings → Student details. Teachers review requests in Notifications. Teachers use the teacher sign-in page after their college provisions access.

How can I get urgent help?

For immediate danger in India, call 112. Elsewhere, call your local emergency number. Contact a trusted person who can stay with you.

For account help, email mannsathi.official@gmail.com. For a problem with the app, send a private bug report.

Terms of Service

Terms for using the MannSathi study workspace

Effective 27 November 2026

Using the service

MannSathi provides study planning, class assignments, focus tools and an optional AI study companion. Use your own account, protect its credentials and respect the privacy of classmates. Teachers may manage only the classes assigned to them.

AI chat eligibility

AI chat is available only to approved participants aged 18 or older in the adult pilot. Check important answers, coursework and deadlines against reliable sources. Use qualified professionals for medical advice and local emergency services for immediate danger.

Responsible use

Share material you have permission to use. Keep passwords, confidential records and other people’s personal details out of prompts. Harassment, impersonation, unauthorized access and attempts to interfere with other users are prohibited. Access may be limited to protect people or service availability.

Account questions and changes

You can stop using MannSathi at any time and request account deletion through the Privacy center. Updated terms will be published here with an effective date. Contact mannsathi.official@gmail.com with questions.

Privacy Policy

What MannSathi processes and who can see it

Effective 27 November 2026 · Contact mannsathi.official@gmail.com

Accounts, classwork and roles

Supabase processes sign-in, email verification and password recovery. MannSathi stores your name, account email and the study data you choose to save, including tasks, calendar events, subjects and focus sessions. When you request enrollment, teachers assigned to that class can see your name, roll number, email, class and division. Teachers can review progress on the work they assign.

Messages and Gemini

When an approved adult pilot member sends a message, MannSathi sends up to 12 recent messages to Google Gemini for token counting and, when allowed, reply generation. Google may receive the text even if the measured request exceeds an allowance. New chats stay in page memory. Previous versions may have saved older conversations on this device. Use Settings → Appearance to manage them.

The pilot requires the operator to confirm a paid Gemini API project. Google’s paid-service terms describe limited logging for safety and legal purposes. Google’s Gemini data terms

Usage and access records

AI administrators can see account-linked request times, model, token counts, outcomes and approved class details. The usage ledger has no columns for prompts or replies. The retention schedule covers 366 UTC calendar days of request and AI audit records, 30 days of quota observations and 24 calendar months of monthly totals and billing snapshots. Administrators operate the scheduled cleanup.

Support and deletion

The private support inbox stores the details you submit, your optional bug-report email or required privacy-request email, and a short-lived pseudonymous rate-limit identifier. Support requests are scheduled for removal after 90 days. Email sent directly to support also reaches the support mailbox. The team uses these details to respond and verify requests.

Ask for access, correction or deletion through the Privacy center or email mannsathi.official@gmail.com. Clearing a chat on your device affects its local copy. When handling a verified deletion request, the team reviews account data, usage records, exports and any applicable retention obligations.

Service providers

Vercel hosts MannSathi, Supabase provides account and database services, and Google processes eligible chat requests. Some pages load the DM Sans typeface from Google Fonts, which receives the network request for the font. Hosting and authentication providers may process network and security logs to operate the service.
